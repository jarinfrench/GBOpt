Phase 1 added deterministic, non-failing geometry diagnostics for both the central and periodic grain-boundary interfaces. The audit was executed across all 197 Zhang UO₂ boundary constructions with no generation failures. It classified 161 structures as suspicious and 36 as acceptable controls, with strong correlation between suspicious classifications and visibly nonuniform interface gaps. Repeated generation of a stratified ten-case cohort produced identical structure hashes and identical audit metrics. Audit classifications remain diagnostic only and do not reject structure generation.
